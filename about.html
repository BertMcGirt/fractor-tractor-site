Our Story

Built by Collectors, for Collectors

Fractor Tractor started with a simple frustration: there was no good way to manage a growing sports card collection digitally. So we built one.

The Problem

Spreadsheets Weren't Cutting It

Like a lot of collectors, we started with spreadsheets. Then shoeboxes of cards with sticky notes. Then a folder of phone photos with no organization at all.

We wanted one place to photograph every card, log grades, track values against actual eBay sales, and share our collections without giving out our home address. Nothing on the market did all of that well.

So we started building Fractor Tractor — a tool made specifically for the way collectors actually work.

Roadmap

Where We're Headed

Here's what we've shipped and what's coming next.

Q1

Core App Launch

Card cataloging, photo uploads, basic search, and collection sharing. The foundation of Fractor Tractor.

Q2

eBay Integration

Live market search, recent sales data, and real-time portfolio valuation powered by eBay's Browse API.

Q3
