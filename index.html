How It Works

Three Steps to a Smarter Collection

Getting started takes less than a minute. Here's how Fractor Tractor works.

1

Add Your Cards

Snap a photo or search our database. Fractor Tractor auto-identifies your cards and pulls in details like game, set, card number, rarity, and condition.

2

Organize & Verify

Sort by game, set, rarity, value, or custom tags. Log PSA, BGS, or CGC grades. Track your collection's total value in real time with live eBay market data.

3

Share or Keep Private

Show off your collection publicly, share with friends, or keep it completely private. You control exactly who sees what.

Features

Built for Serious Collectors

Everything you need to manage, value, and enjoy your trading card game collection in one place.

🔍

eBay Market Search

Search eBay's live marketplace directly from Fractor Tractor. See current listings, recent sales, and real-time market values for any card.

📷

Photo Cataloging

Upload photos of the front and back of every card. Build a visual library of your entire collection.

✅

Grade Verification

Log PSA, BGS, CGC, and other grading service results. Verified grades are displayed on your cards and boost collection credibility.

📈

Portfolio Tracking

See your collection's total value update in real time. Track individual card performance and spot trends in your portfolio.

🔒

Privacy Controls

Share your collection publicly, with select friends, or keep it entirely private. Granular controls at the collection and card level.

📱

Mobile-First Design

Add cards on the go from card shows and shops. Fractor Tractor works beautifully on any device.
