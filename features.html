Powerful Features for Serious Collectors

From snapping a photo to tracking your portfolio's value, Fractor Tractor gives you every tool to manage your sports card collection.

Core Features

The Foundation of Your Digital Collection

🔍

eBay Market Search

Search eBay's live marketplace directly from your dashboard. See active listings, completed sales, and price trends for any card. Powered by eBay's Browse API with real-time data.

📷

HD Photo Cataloging

Upload high-resolution photos of the front and back of every card. Zoom, compare, and build a visual library that represents your collection exactly as it is.

✅

Fractor Tractor Verification

Prove your cards are really in your hands. Snap photos directly from your device for manual review by our team — verified cards carry a badge everyone can trust.

📈

Real-Time Portfolio Value

Your collection's total value updates automatically based on recent eBay sales data. Track individual card performance over time with historical charts.

🏷

Smart Organization

Organize cards by sport, year, set, player, team, or custom tags. Create multiple collections — one for basketball, one for vintage, one for investment-grade.

🔒

Granular Privacy Controls

Share individual collections publicly, with select friends via a private link, or keep everything completely private. Control visibility at the collection and card level.

Advanced Tools

Go Beyond the Basics

📊

Market Analytics

Spot trends before they happen. See which cards are gaining value, which are cooling off, and which players are trending in the market.

🔔

Price Alerts

Set alerts for specific cards or players. Get notified when a card you're watching drops below your target price or when a rare listing appears on eBay.

🔁

Bulk Import

Moving from spreadsheets or another platform? Import your entire collection via CSV. We'll match your data to our card database automatically.

🔗

Shareable Collection Links

Generate a beautiful, browsable link to your collection. Perfect for showing potential buyers, trading partners, or fellow collectors what you've got.

📱

Mobile-First Design

Add cards on the go at card shows, shops, and meetups. The mobile experience is built to be fast and seamless — scan, snap, and save in seconds.

🛠

API Access (Coming Soon)

Build on top of your collection data. Our upcoming API will let you integrate Fractor Tractor with your own tools, storefronts, and workflows.

Spotlight

Fractor Tractor Verification

Anyone can type a card into a spreadsheet. Verification proves the card is actually in your hands. To verify a card, you capture photos of it directly with your device's camera — no gallery uploads — and they're sent to our team for manual review.

  • ✓
    Capture Live, In App

    Photos are taken directly with your device at the moment of submission, so what we review is the card in front of you — not an image pulled from the internet.

  • ✓
    Manual Review

    Every submission is checked by our team. When it passes, the card earns a verified badge that displays wherever your collection is shared.

  • ✓
    Proof of Possession

    A verified card tells buyers, traders, and fellow collectors that the card you've cataloged is genuinely in your collection.

  • ✓
    Clear Ownership of Serialized Cards

    For numbered cards, verification makes ownership unambiguous — no one else can claim they hold your 1/1.
