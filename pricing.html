FAQ

Common Questions

Can I try Pro before committing?

Yes! Every new account gets a 14-day free trial of Pro with full access to all features. No credit card required.

What happens to my cards if I downgrade?

Your cards are always safe. If you downgrade from Pro to Starter, you keep all your cards and collections — there's no card limit on the free plan. You just won't be able to verify additional cards beyond Starter's 50-verification limit until you upgrade again.

How does the eBay purchase import work?

Connect your eBay account and Fractor Tractor pulls in the cards you've purchased, matching them to our card database automatically — no manual data entry needed to get your collection up to date.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are no contracts or cancellation fees. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings, and you'll retain access through the end of your billing period.

Do you support grading services other than PSA?

Yes! We support PSA, BGS (Beckett), SGC, CGC, and more. You can log grades from any grading service and they'll display on your cards.
